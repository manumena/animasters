# Initial homepage directions

Created October 28–29, 2026. Archived October 29.

[Open preview](preview.html) · [Editable source](source.html)

Variants:

- **Anime arcade:** competitive split hero, vivid accent and angled sample round.
- **Cozy anime hangout:** warm palette, illustrated listening corner and an invitation to game night.
- **Clean music quiz:** centered headline and actions, followed by a wide sample round and concise instructions.

Owner feedback: Clean music quiz is preferred. Anime arcade resembles Melomatch too closely. The next exploration adds the existing anime-style lo-fi café background to the preferred direction.

The sketches use native layout and decorative shapes rather than external artwork. Preview interactions are local demonstrations; they do not create rooms, submit real guesses or play audio.
